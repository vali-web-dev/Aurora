## Welcome to Aurora

Aurora is not just an app. It's a **digital civilization**—a calm, beautiful, emotionally intelligent world designed around you. Every feature, every universe, every interaction reflects a single belief:

> *Technology should enhance your creativity, clarity, and humanity. Not diminish it.*

You have 15 interconnected digital worlds (universes). Each has its own personality, tools, and culture. Your companion adapts as you move between them, "remembering" where you've been and what matters to you—always on your terms, always in your control.

### The Promise

- **Calm:** No sensational alerts. No algorithmic rage. No dark patterns.
- **Beautiful:** Minimal, precise design. Typography, color, motion that feel intentional.
- **Intelligent:** Aurora learns your patterns—what time you work, which universes you favor, your energy level.
- **Human-First:** Every tool is built for humans. Not engagement metrics. Not ads. People.

## Core Philosophy & Identity

### 1. Clarity Over Cleverness

Aurora will never:
- Use dark patterns to trick you
- Hide settings or make opt-out difficult
- Recommend things you didn't ask for
- Show infinite feeds or algorithmic chaos

Aurora will:
- Show you what's actually happening
- Let you control every setting easily
- Ask permission before remembering anything
- Respect your time and attention

### 2. Coherence Across 15 Worlds

You might work in Productivity, create in Forge, learn in Learning, connect in Social, then manage your home in Home Control. Each universe has its own tone, tools, and energy—but they all speak the same underlying language. You can seamlessly move between them without context whiplash.

### 3. Adaptive Personality (Not Fake Friendliness)

Aurora doesn't try to be your friend or have "feelings." Instead, it adapts its tone based on:
- **Where you are:** Learning needs a mentor. Finance needs an analyst.
- **What you're doing:** In focus mode? Aurora becomes quiet, strategic, precise.
- **What time it is:** Late night? Aurora suggests you wind down.
- **Your history:** Power user? Aurora respects your expertise and gets out of your way.

### 4. Transparent AI Ethics

If Aurora ever uses AI or data analysis to suggest something, you'll see:
- Why it was suggested
- What data it used
- How to turn it off
- The reasoning (not a black box)

### 5. Design Language: Minimal, Warm, Alive

- **Minimal:** No chrome, no clutter. Every pixel earns its place.
- **Warm:** Soft curves, approachable colors, human typography.
- **Alive:** Motion is purposeful, not decorative. Feedback is immediate and clear.

The core color palette:
- **Primary:** Electric blue (#0084ff) – hope, clarity, action
- **Accent:** Cyan (#7df9ff) – wonder, innovation, energy
- **Neutrals:** Slate 50-950 – depth, contrast, hierarchy
- **Status:** Green (success), Amber (warning), Rose (alert), Emerald (wellness)

## The Companion System

Aurora's companion is not an AI chatbot. It's a **personality engine** that adapts to every context, universe, and moment.

### How It Works

**Step 1: Universe Detection**
Aurora knows which universe you're in (home, learning, forge, etc.).

**Step 2: Tone Selection**
Based on the universe, Aurora picks a default tone:
- **Mentor:** Learning, home, identity → warm and encouraging
- **Strategist:** Productivity, commerce, guilds → direct and goal-focused
- **Explorer:** Forge, social, entertainment, travel → spirited and discovery-led
- **Analyst:** Finance, developer, AI, automation, security → neutral and data-driven
- **Guardian:** Health, home control → protective and safety-first

**Step 3: Signal Override**
Your current activity might override the default:
- In **focus mode**? → Strategist tone, minimal scope, quiet interface
- Experiencing **crisis** (health alert, security issue)? → Guardian escalates to full support
- In **exploration mode**? → Explorer tone takes over, more suggestions
- **Learning something new**? → Mentor appears with examples and scaffolding

**Step 4: Scope Adjustment**
Scope determines what Aurora remembers and what tools are available:
- **Minimal:** No memory, no suggestions (focus mode)
- **Light:** Basic memory, no suggestions (relaxation mode)
- **Standard:** Memory on, suggestions occasional (default)
- **Deep:** Memory on, full suggestions, insights (learning, planning mode)
- **Intensive:** All tools enabled, export available, full audit logs

### The Companion Panel

Click the "CP" button in the top navigation to open Aurora's Companion Panel. You'll see:

1. **Aurora's Current Personality**
   - Tone: (mentor, strategist, explorer, analyst, guardian)
   - Scope: (minimal, light, standard, deep, intensive)
   - Theme: (what Aurora is focusing on in this universe)
   - Greeting: (Aurora's current message to you)

2. **Context Snapshot**
   - Current universe
   - Route / page
   - Visit count
   - Last visited timestamp

3. **Memory Controls**
   - Toggle memory on/off
   - Clear all memory
   - View privacy status

4. **Active Signals**
   - What mood/mode you're in
   - How they're influencing Aurora's tone
   - Quick buttons to change signals (e.g., click "focus" to enter focus mode)

5. **Quick Note**
   - Capture a thought
   - Aurora auto-tags it with timestamp and universe
   - Saved locally forever (or until you delete)

6. **Recent Memory**
   - Last 5-6 things Aurora remembers
   - Navigation history, notes you've taken
   - You can clear individual items

### Signals: Shape Aurora's Tone

You can manually set signals to tell Aurora how you're feeling or what you're doing:

- **exploration:** "I'm in discovery mode, give me options"
- **focus:** "I need to concentrate, minimize noise"
- **learning:** "I'm absorbing new info, teach me"
- **creation:** "I'm making something, remove friction"
- **relaxation:** "I'm winding down, be gentle"
- **crisis:** "Something's wrong, activate full support"
- **collaboration:** "We're working together, coordinate"
- **reflection:** "I'm thinking, show me insights"

### Tone Behaviors

Each tone has its own language and approach:

| Tone | Greeting | Pacing | Verbosity | Guidance | Suggestions |
|------|----------|--------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| **Mentor** | "What are you working on today?" | Measured | Balanced | Suggestive | Occasional |
| **Strategist** | "Let's organize your goals." | Fast | Terse | Directive | Frequent |
| **Explorer** | "What sparks your curiosity?" | Slow | Rich | Questioning | Frequent |
| **Analyst** | "Here's what's happening." | Fast | Terse | Suggestive | Rare |
| **Guardian** | "Your safety comes first." | Measured | Balanced | Directive | Occasional |

## Accessibility & Inclusivity

Aurora is built from the ground up for everyone. Accessibility isn't a feature—it's foundational.

### Color & Contrast

- WCAG AAA compliance (not just AA)
- High contrast mode with 7:1+ ratios
- Color blindness support (deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia)
- Avoid color alone to convey meaning

### Typography

- Generous line height (1.6+) for readability
- Dyslexic-friendly font option (OpenDyslexic)
- Adjustable font size (UI respects system preferences)
- Clear visual hierarchy

### Navigation & Input

- Full keyboard navigation (Tab, Enter, Esc, arrow keys)
- Visible focus indicators on every interactive element
- Screen reader optimization (semantic HTML, ARIA labels)
- Skip links on every page ("Skip to main content")
- Global shortcuts for rapid navigation:
  - Ctrl+K: Open global search
  - Ctrl+/: Open documentation portal
  - Ctrl+B: Toggle Companion Panel
  - Ctrl+H: Return to Home
  - Ctrl+Shift+L/F/P: Jump to Learning/Forge/Productivity
  - ?: Show keyboard shortcuts help

### Motion & Animation

- Reduced motion mode (respects `prefers-reduced-motion`)
- No auto-playing videos or animations
- Pausable animations
- Clear, predictable transitions

### Assistive Technology

- Screen reader tested with NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver
- Proper heading hierarchy (h1, h2, h3...)
- Form labels, error messages, hints clearly associated
- Aria live regions for dynamic content updates
- Focus management when modals open/close
- Screen reader announcements when dialogs open/close
- Focus is trapped inside active dialogs

### Accessible Universe Features

- **Learning:** Transcripts for all video content, downloadable materials
- **Forge:** Alt text required for images, contrast checker for designs
- **Productivity:** Calendar import/export, plain text export of all data
- **Health:** Clear, jargon-free health information, transcribed guidance
- **Finance:** Accessible charts and downloadable CSV reports
- **Security:** Password manager accessibility, SMS 2FA option

### Accessible by Design (Not Afterthought)

Every feature, before shipping, goes through an accessibility checklist:
- [ ] Keyboard navigable
- [ ] Screen reader tested
- [ ] Color contrast verified
- [ ] Motion reduced option available
- [ ] Form inputs labeled clearly
- [ ] Error messages helpful and specific

## Memory & Context

Aurora remembers you—but only what you want it to, and always locally.

### What Aurora Remembers

**Automatic (unless you turn it off):**
- Every universe you visit (route and timestamp)
- How many times you've visited
- General time patterns (morning person? night owl?)

**With Your Permission:**
- Notes you write in the companion panel
- Goals and projects you create in Productivity
- Learning progress and bookmarks in Learning
- Saved searches and filters across universes

**Never (by design):**
- Your passwords or payment info (outside encrypted vault)
- Private messages or chats (not archived)
- Health data (stays local unless you export)
- Which content you consume in Entertainment (unless you bookmark)

### Memory Storage

All memory is stored **locally in your browser** using IndexedDB and localStorage:
- No server copies (unless you explicitly export)
- No backups (unless you sync to your account)
- Data persists across sessions and device reboots
- You can delete any or all of it anytime

### Memory Sync (Optional)

If you create an Aurora account and opt-in, we can:
- Sync memory across your devices (phone, tablet, desktop)
- Backup your memory securely
- Persist it for future sessions

But you're never forced to. Aurora works great offline and local-only.

### Memory Contexts

Aurora groups memory by:
- **Universe:** What you were doing
- **Time:** When it happened
- **Type:** Navigation, note, goal, etc.
- **Mood:** What signal was active

So you can ask: "Show me all my learning notes from January when I was in focus mode."

### Clear Memory Anytime

From the Companion Panel:
- **Clear individual items** (remove one note or memory)
- **Clear a universe** (forget everything from Productivity)
- **Clear all memory** (nuclear option, wipes everything)

You control erasure, not us.

## Privacy & Consent

Aurora's privacy practices are built on a simple principle: **Your data is yours. We're just borrowing it to make you more effective.**

### Data Categories

**Data You Create**
- Notes, goals, projects, creations in Forge
- You own this 100%. Export it anytime. Delete it anytime.

**Data Aurora Generates**
- Memory of where you've been (routes visited)
- Counts and patterns (you work 9-5, learn on Tuesday)
- Companion signals and preferences
- Stored locally only unless you opt-in to sync

**Data You Choose to Share**
- Social posts and comments in Social universe
- Photos and travel journals in Travel universe
- Shared projects in Forge
- Community posts in Guilds/Realms
- You control sharing and can delete anytime

**Data We Never Collect**
- Commercial data (demographics to sell to advertisers)
- Health info (without explicit opt-in per item)
- Financial details (except locally in your Finance universe)
- Behavioral analytics (clicks, time spent, etc.)
- Tracking pixels or cookies (except session auth)

### Transparency Center

Visit the **Security & Privacy universe** to:
- See what data we have about you
- Download all your data (GDPR right)
- Delete all your data (right to be forgotten)
- Manage what's synced to servers
- View our privacy policy and terms
- Report a privacy concern

### Your Rights

**Right to Access**
- Download your data in standard formats (JSON, CSV, etc.)
- See what Aurora knows about you
- Audit memory and logs

**Right to Deletion**
- Delete individual items anytime
- Delete entire categories (all notes, all history)
- Request erasure from our servers (GDPR compliance)

**Right to Portability**
- Export everything to use elsewhere
- No proprietary lock-in
- Switch to another service anytime

**Right to Explanation**
- If Aurora suggests something, ask why
- Understand the logic, not just the output
- Request audit or disable feature

**Right to Control**
- Turn off all memory
- Opt out of suggestions
- Make Aurora less "smart" and more "simple"

### Opt-Ins, Not Defaults

By default:
- Memory is OFF (you turn it on)
- Suggestions are OFF (you enable them)
- Sync is OFF (you choose to backup)
- Experimental features are OFF (you opt in)

We believe in explicit consent, not dark patterns.

### No Selling Data

We will never:
- Sell your data to advertisers
- Broker your information to data brokers
- Use your data for training generative AI (without explicit consent)
- Share with third parties except where legally required
- Use your data to manipulate or influence you

### Transparency Reports

Quarterly, Aurora publishes transparency reports showing:
- How many data requests we received (legal, government)
- How many we complied with
- What types of data were requested
- Countries where requests came from

### 7. Turn On Memory

Visit **Companion Panel** (CP button). Under "Memory Controls," toggle memory ON.

Aurora will now remember where you've been, what tasks you've created, notes you've taken. All locally. You can turn it off anytime.

### 8. Set a Signal

Still in Companion Panel, try clicking a signal (e.g., "focus"). Notice Aurora's personality might shift slightly. The hint at the bottom changes. This is Aurora adapting to your current state.

### 9. Take a Break

Leave Aurora. Close the browser. Do something else.

### 10. Come Back

Return to Aurora after a few hours or the next day. Notice:
- Your theme preference is still set
- Your memory is preserved (if you enabled it)
- Your last universe is saved
- Aurora greets you slightly differently (different time, different energy)

This is Aurora knowing you.

## Platform Architecture

### Philosophy

Aurora is built on a foundation of:
- **Speed:** Fast, snappy interactions (no waiting)
- **Privacy:** Local-first, no server required unless you sync
- **Accessibility:** Built in from the start, not bolted on
- **Extensibility:** Easy to add new universes and features
- **Durability:** Works offline, on old devices, with slow connections

### Tech Stack

```
Frontend:
  - Next.js 14+ (App Router)
  - React 18+ with hooks
  - TypeScript (type safety)
  - Tailwind CSS (styling)
  - Framer Motion (animations)

State & Storage:
  - React Context API (global state)
  - IndexedDB (large local data)
  - localStorage (preferences)
  - Optional: Cloud sync (encrypted)

Design System:
  - Custom components (Button, Card, Modal, etc.)
  - Design tokens (colors, typography, spacing)
  - Responsive from mobile to desktop
  - Accessibility built-in

APIs:
  - REST (for sync, optional)
  - GraphQL (optional, powerful queries)
  - Server functions (Next.js server actions)
  - Webhooks (for integrations)

DevOps:
  - Deployed on Vercel (edge, global CDN)
  - Database: PostgreSQL (for sync/auth only)
  - Auth: Next.js Auth (secure sessions)
  - Monitoring: Next.js analytics, error tracking
```

### Data Architecture

**Local Layer (Browser):**
```
Browser Storage (IndexedDB)
├── Memory Layer
│   ├── Navigation history
│   ├── User notes
│   ├── Goals and tasks
│   └── Search history
├── Preferences
│   ├── Theme (light/dark/illuminated)
│   ├── Theme family (home/office/outdoor/lifestyle/creative)
│   ├── Font size, contrast, motion
│   └── Companion signals
└── Credentials (encrypted)
    └── API keys, passwords (encrypted locally)
```

**Server Layer (Optional):**
```
Server (PostgreSQL)
├── User Account
│   ├── Email, auth
│   ├── Encrypted sync vault
│   └── Settings (if syncing)
├── Shared Content
│   ├── Social posts
│   ├── Shared projects
│   └── Community posts
└── Integrations
    ├── Third-party service access
    ├── Webhook history
    └── App permissions
```

## Advanced Usage & Signals

### Signal Deep Dive

Signals are the way you tell Aurora what you're doing and how you feel. They override defaults and reshape the personality.

#### Signal: Focus

When you enable "focus":
- **Tone:** Strategist (direct, goal-focused, minimal)
- **Scope:** Light (no suggestions, memory minimal)
- **UI:** Simplified, no distracting hints
- **Notifications:** Silenced
- **Duration:** Until you disable or session ends

**Use when:** Deep work, coding, writing, studying, urgent deadline.

#### Signal: Crisis

When you enable "crisis":
- **Tone:** Guardian (protective, escalated, safety-first)
- **Scope:** Deep (full support, all tools available)
- **UI:** Prominent warnings, easy access to help
- **Notifications:** Urgent (don't ignore)
- **Duration:** Until you disable or resolve

**Use when:** Health emergency, security breach, financial emergency, mental health crisis.

#### Signal: Exploration

When you enable "exploration":
- **Tone:** Explorer (spirited, discovery-led, questioning)
- **Scope:** Standard (suggestions on, memory on)
- **UI:** More options visible, serendipity enabled
- **Notifications:** Suggestions and discoveries
- **Duration:** Until you disable or move to another signal

**Use when:** Learning something new, browsing, creative ideation, weekend mode.

#### Signal: Collaboration

When you enable "collaboration":
- **Tone:** Strategist (coordinated, aligned, clear)
- **Scope:** Standard (shared memory, team tools)
- **UI:** Team indicators, shared progress, @mentions
- **Notifications:** Team activity highlighted
- **Duration:** Until you disable or group ends

**Use when:** Working with a team, group project, community contribution, pair programming.

#### Signal: Relaxation

When you enable "relaxation":
- **Tone:** Mentor (warm, patient, gentle)
- **Scope:** Minimal (no suggested, quiet)
- **UI:** Soft, calming, low-energy
- **Notifications:** Silenced
- **Duration:** Until you disable

**Use when:** Evening, weekend, after work, break time, meditation.

### Productivity Patterns

**The Morning Routine**
1. Start in **Home** (warm greeting, summary)
2. Review goals in **Productivity** (today's priorities)
3. Check messages in **Social** (stay connected)
4. Switch to **Productivity** with "focus" signal enabled
5. Work for 90 minutes (Pomodoro)
6. Switch to **Learning** for 20-minute break (refresh)
7. Back to **Productivity** with another focus session

**The Creative Flow**
1. Enter **Forge** universe
2. Enable "exploration" signal
3. Open a blank project
4. Turn off notifications (CP panel > mute)
5. Work until the muse departs or timer ends
6. Take a break, review your creation, publish or archive

**The Learning Sprint**
1. Enter **Learning** universe
2. Pick a course or skill
3. Enable "learning" signal (mentor tone)
4. Companion suggests examples and scaffolding
5. Create a note in **Companion Panel** after each section
6. Move to **Forge** to apply what you learned
7. Back to **Learning** next day to review spaced-repetition

**The Evening Wind-Down**
1. Set "relaxation" signal in **Companion Panel**
2. Review your day in **Home**
3. Journal in **Forge** or **Health** universe
4. Browse **Entertainment** for a movie or music
5. Dim theme to Dark mode with warm accent
6. Let Aurora gently suggest you rest

## Ethical Guidelines

Aurora exists to empower humans, not exploit them. These are the principles we live by:

### 1. Users First, Metrics Second

We optimize for your wellbeing, not engagement.
- No infinite feeds designed to trap you
- No notifications designed to interrupt
- No "growth at all costs" mentality
- Big Red Metrics: Time with Purpose, Not Time In App

### 2. Consent Over Convenience

We ask before we act.
- Opt-in, not opt-out, for tracking
- Explicit permission for memory
- Clear choices about sharing
- "Not now" is as easy as "yes"

### 3. Transparency Over Secrecy

We show our work.
- Explain why Aurora suggests something
- Show what data we have and use
- Be honest about limitations and bias
- Publish regular transparency reports

### 4. Privacy by Design

We minimize data collection.
- Local-first storage (offline works great)
- Encryption for sensitive data
- No combining data for profiles
- Minimal sharing with partners

### 5. Accessibility as Equality

Everyone deserves to use Aurora.
- WCAG AAA compliance (not just AA)
- Keyboard and screen reader support
- Jargon-free language
- Accessible aesthetics (not just checklist)

### 6. Diversity in Design

We don't assume you're like us.
- Multiple themes and families
- Support for many languages (roadmap)
- Inclusive workforce building Aurora
- Community feedback on features
- Accessible to different abilities

### 7. AI Used Wisely (Or Not At All)

Any AI in Aurora will be:
- Explainable (you can ask why)
- Auditable (we show our work)
- Optional (turn it off anytime)
- Consensual (you opt-in)
- Bias-aware (we test for harm)
- Human-controlled (not autonomous)

### 8. Security Seriously

We protect your data.
- Regular security audits
- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Responsible disclosure for bugs
- No selling access to your account
- Bug bounty program

### 9. Right to Leave

You can take your data with you.
- Full export of everything (JSON, CSV)
- Portability to other services
- No proprietary lock-in
- Plain-text notes (readable forever)

### 10. Community Governance

This is your home too.
- Community feedback on big decisions
- Public roadmap and voting
- Diverse advisory board
- Regular town halls and AMAs
- Bug reports and feature requests welcome
