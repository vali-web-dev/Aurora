Advanced workflows for experienced Aurora users who want to unlock deep capabilities, automation chains, and complex multi-system orchestration.

## Scenario 1: Autonomous Research & Learning Machine

**Goal**: Run a continuous, self-directed research project that spans 3 months with minimal manual intervention.

### Setup Phase (Week 1)

#### Create Automation Chain
1. **Automation**: Build workflow "Daily Research Digest"
   - **Trigger**: Time-based (9:00 AM daily)
   - **Action 1**: Search news APIs for keywords (your research topic)
   - **Action 2**: Summarize articles with AI
   - **Action 3**: Create task in Productivity with summary
   - **Action 4**: Post best article to private Collections blog
   - **Action 5**: Send weekly digest to Communities research channel

#### Link Learning
1. **Learning**: Enroll in 2-3 courses on research topic
2. **Create**: Start research journal (shared Create document)
3. **Productivity**: Create weekly milestone "Synthesize findings"

#### Set Data Flows
1. **Finance**: Budget for research tools/subscriptions
2. **Health**: Log "Research mode" signal to avoid burnout
3. **Companion Panel**: Set to "Researcher" tone (focused, analytical)

### Execution Phase (Weeks 2-12)

#### Week 1-4: Gather & Learn
- Morning: Automation feeds you digests
- 9-11 AM: Learning course + apply notes to research journal in Create
- 11 AM-12 PM: Review digest, add link to Collections
- 12-1 PM: Communities research channel (respond to others' questions)
- Weekly: Synthesize findings (Productivity milestone)

#### Week 5-8: Deepen & Connect
- Automation still running; you now see patterns
- Create research timeline (Create doc with images, links)
- Communities: Post your synthesis; get expert feedback
- Productivity: New tasks from feedback (refine methodology, etc.)
- Learning: Take advanced course on specific subtopic

#### Week 9-12: Publish & Share
- Automation feeds into Collections blog
- Create: Finalize research paper or presentation
- Productivity: Final synthesis task (week 12)
- Social: Share research findings with network
- Communities: Present findings to research community

#### Output
- Research journal (Create document, fully cited)
- 12 weeks of digests (Automation archive)
- Blog posts on findings (Collections)
- Credentials from Learning courses
- Community reputation from contributions

### Power User Techniques

**1. Conditional Automation**
```
IF new_article_relevance_score > 0.8
THEN create task with HIGH priority
ELSE add to weekly digest, not immediate
```

**2. Data Sink to Multiple Universes**
- Single research finding flows to:
  - Collections (blog post)
  - Productivity (as task context)
  - Communities (for discussion)
  - Learning (as case study)
  - Create (as research note)

**3. Weekly Auto-Synthesis Loop**
- Automation collects week's findings
- Creates Productivity task: "Synthesize week's topic"
- Task has template with data from digests
- Your notes auto-link to sources in Automation logs
- Result becomes Collections post template

**4. Burnout Detection**
- Health signal tracks research intensity
- If stress goes up, Learning pauses automation (gives you rest)
- Automation resumes after 3 days if stress down
- Prevents research rabbit holes

**Result**: 12 weeks of continuous, semi-autonomous research. You guide, Aurora organizes.

## Scenario 2: Multi-Person Product Development Sprint

**Goal**: Run a 2-week agile sprint with 5 people across 3 time zones, minimal meetings.

### Pre-Sprint Setup

#### Automation Orchestration
1. **Automation Workflows** (run on schedule):
   - **Daily 6 AM UTC**: Sprint standup template created in Communities
   - **Daily 9 AM, 2 PM, 5 PM UTC**: Team chat reminder (via Social scheduled posts)
   - **Every 4 hours**: Check Productivity milestones; update Communities progress board
   - **Friday 5 PM UTC**: Sprint review prep (collect stats, compile metrics)

#### Brand & Alignment
1. **Brand**: Sprint-specific brand guidelines pinned
2. **Productivity**: Master project with milestones:
   - Design complete (Monday EOD)
   - Dev done (Friday EOD)
   - QA pass (Friday 5 PM)

#### Communities Setup
1. **Communities**: Create private team space "Q1 Sprint 2"
2. Create channels:
   - `#standup` (automation populates daily)
   - `#decisions` (brand alignment checks here)
   - `#blockers` (auto-escalates if unresolved > 24 hrs)

### Async-First Execution

#### Monday: Kickoff
- **Automation**: Posts sprint goals from Productivity
- **Design Lead**: Uploads design in Create (shared doc)
- **Communities**: Design review thread starts; everyone comments async
- **Productivity**: Tasks auto-assigned (via Automation to team)
- **No sync meeting needed** (async replaces kickoff call)

#### Tuesday-Thursday: Development
- **Daily**: Each person posts 30-sec standup (text/voice) in Communities
- **Create**: Designers iterate on feedback; version history tracks all
- **Automation**: Every 4 hours, compiles comments into dev tasks
- **Productivity**: Dev team pulls tasks, marks done as they ship
- **Communities**: Decisions logged and timestamped (async approval)
- **Health**: Signal warns if someone working > 10 hrs/day (burnout prevention)

#### Friday: Sprint End
- **Automation**: Friday 9 AM compiles week's metrics
  - Tasks completed: 23/25
  - Design iterations: 6
  - Code commits: 47
  - Communities decisions: 12
- **Productivity**: Sprint review task auto-populates with metrics
- **Communities**: Team reviews together async; discussions link to Create files
- **Social**: Team celebrates week (internal post to team Social space)
- **Finance**: If budget tracking enabled, team sees sprint cost

#### Saturday: Retro (Only sync of week)
- **Communities**: Async retro
  - "What went well?" thread
  - "What to improve?" thread
  - "Action items for next sprint" thread
- **Results auto-link to next sprint planning**

### Power User Techniques

**1. Automation Workflow Chain**
```
Every 4 hours:
  ├─ Check Productivity for new tasks
  ├─ Auto-create Communities thread if > 2 new tasks
  ├─ Alert team in Social (@here mention)
  ├─ Update Brand compliance checklist (auto-check design files)
  └─ Push metrics to shared Create dashboard
```

**2. Brand Compliance Automation**
- Automation scans Create files for brand violations
- Posts to Communities `#brand-check` channel
- Requires sign-off before shipping
- Prevents design QA delays

**3. Async Decision Recording**
- Communities thread starts
- Voting reactions (👍 = yes, 👎 = no)
- After 24 hrs (or unanimous), auto-resolved
- Decision logged with timestamp, approvers, rationale
- Linked to Productivity task for implementation

**4. Burnout Early Warning**
- Health signal from each team member (self-reported daily)
- Automation detects: stress ↑ + sleep ↓ + productivity ↑↑
- Posts to `#wellbeing` channel (private, team only)
- Suggests workload rebalancing
- If person marks "need break", their Productivity pauses task assignment

**Result**: 2-week sprint, 1 sync meeting (retro), rest fully async. No context switching. All decisions documented.

## Scenario 3: Digital Nomad with Offline-First Sync

**Goal**: Travel for 6 months across 15 countries with unreliable internet; stay fully productive and synced.

### Preparation Phase (2 Weeks Before)

#### Comprehensive Local Sync
1. **All Universes**: Download everything
   - Travel: All maps, offline hotel/restaurant data
   - Learning: All courses (video/transcript offline)
   - Entertainment: Movies/podcasts for flights
   - Create: All project files (high-res versions)
   - Finance: Full transaction history (for reference)
   - Health: Full health history (logs, trends)

2. **Automation**: Setup sync workflows
   - **Weekly**: When WiFi available, sync all universes
   - **Conflict resolution**: Local changes always win; cloud version merges after
   - **Sync status**: Dashboard shows if fully synced

3. **Companion Panel**: Configure offline mode
   - Suggestions work offline (cached AI models)
   - Search works offline (on-device index)
   - Sync queued; executes when online

#### Pre-Load Data
1. **Finance**: Set budgets for 6 months by location
2. **Productivity**: Create tasks for entire trip (by week/location)
3. **Travel**: Map out rough itinerary (flexible)
4. **Health**: Log baseline metrics before travel
5. **Communities**: Download important threads (forums, guides)

### Travel Phase (6 Months)

#### Typical Week
- **Monday**: Arrive new city
  - Open Travel: Offline map shows hotels, restaurants, transit
  - Add local tasks to Productivity (visit market, museum, etc.)
  - Health: Log arrival mood, jet lag level
  - Offline: No need for internet

- **Tuesday-Thursday**: Explore
  - Travel journal: Add daily photos and notes (all local)
  - Create: Sketch or document observations (offline)
  - Learning: Continue courses (offline video)
  - Health: Log daily mood, sleep, activity

- **Friday**: Work/Catch-up Day
  - If WiFi available: Sync all universes
    - Travel notes uploaded (but priority to new locations)
    - Create sketches synced to cloud as backup
    - Learning progress confirmed
    - Finance transactions synced (real-time if online banking available)
  - If no WiFi: Keep working offline; sync next opportunity

- **Weekend**: Reflect
  - Productivity: Weekly review (even offline)
  - Health: Weekly trend analysis (local data)
  - Social: Write posts (queue for sync)
  - Learning: Week assessment

#### Critical Power User Setup

**1. Intelligent Sync Strategy**
```
When WiFi detected:
├─ Priority 1 (sync immediately)
│  ├─ Finance (transactions, verify balance)
│  ├─ Health (location-based data matters)
│  └─ Messages (stay connected with team/loved ones)
│
├─ Priority 2 (sync after priority 1)
│  ├─ Travel journals and photos
│  ├─ Create work (backup)
│  └─ Learning progress
│
└─ Priority 3 (low bandwidth)
   ├─ Social posts (cache-friendly)
   └─ Streaming content (Entertainment) - skip if bandwidth low
```

**2. Predictive Offline Capability**
- Before entering remote area: download for 1 week offline
- Automation detects your location leaving coverage area
- Suggests pre-load of maps, courses, entertainment
- One-click "Go Offline" downloads everything needed

**3. Local-First Create Workflow**
- All edits happen locally (zero latency, works offline)
- When online: Choose version to sync (yours or cloud's)
- Drafts accumulate; you choose when to publish
- No "lost work" anxiety despite connectivity gaps

**4. Budget Tracking with Offline Finance**
- Track expenses in local currency (with real-time exchange cached)
- When online: Convert to home currency, sync transactions
- Automation runs weekly budget check (even offline, uses cached budget)
- Alerts if you're ahead/behind on weekly allocation

#### Monthly Rhythm
- **Day 1-7**: Explore new location
- **Day 8**: If internet: do full sync day (backup everything)
- **Day 9-30**: Deep exploration, light work/learning
- **Day 30**: Reflect on month (Productivity + Health)

### Special Power Techniques

**1. Device Failure Recovery**
- Every 2 weeks: Manual backup to encrypted USB stick
- If device fails: Restore from last backup
- Sync re-syncs any new data post-backup
- No total data loss

**2. Collaborative Nomading**
- If traveling with team:
  - Create shared documents (local edits sync when online)
  - Productivity: Shared project visible even offline
  - Communities: Offline forum (sync when online resolves conflicts)
  - Health: Team wellness check-ins (queued)

**3. Location-Based Automation**
- Aurora learns your patterns
- In location A: Suggest restaurants you liked last visit
- In location B: Remind about visa deadline (based on Travel)
- Automation works offline (location-cached)

**4. Mood & Location Correlation**
- Health signal: Log mood + location (coordjnates)
- Over 6 months: See which places energize you, drain you
- Create travel style profile: "Beach cities best for me", "Mountains boring"
- Next trip: Automation suggests locations based on historical mood data

#### Result
- 6 months fully offline-capable
- Zero data loss despite unreliable internet
- Continuous work, learning, creativity, and wellness tracking
- Return home with full data history, synchronized across cloud

## Scenario 4: Creative Output Machine (2000 Hours → 50 Finished Works)

**Goal**: Over 2 years, produce 50 finished creative works (music, writing, design, video) with automated feedback, iteration tracking, and community engagement.

### Year 1: Foundation & Habit

#### Automation Setup
1. **Daily Creative Ritual** (Automation):
   - 6 AM: Timer started (120 min focus block in Productivity)
   - 6 AM: Companion Panel set to "Creative" tone
   - 6 AM-8 AM: You create (no distractions)
   - 8 AM: Log creativity session to Health (log "creative work")
   - Weekly: Compile works in Create

2. **Weekly Feedback Loop** (Automation):
   - Friday 5 PM: Auto-export week's best works (Create)
   - Create a private "Works in Progress" Collections post
   - Tag 5 trusted friends in Social
   - Collect feedback comments
   - Compile feedback into Monday task (review feedback + iterate)

#### Learning Integration
1. **Learning**: Skill-building courses (1 per month)
   - Beats/composition (Month 1)
   - Mixing & production (Month 2)
   - etc.
2. **Create**: Apply lesson from course immediately
3. **Productivity**: Habit tracking (daily creative session)

#### Health Monitoring
1. **Health**: Track creative energy
   - Log mood before/after creating
   - Track sleep (correlates with creativity)
   - Track "flow state" moments (when did you lose track of time?)
2. **Automation**: If flow rate drops 2 weeks, alert you

### Year 1 Output
- 156 creative sessions (120-min blocks)
- 52 "Works in Progress" (1 per week)
- ~12 completed works
- Steady learning progression
- Community feedback integrated

### Year 2: Acceleration & Polish

#### Audience Building
1. **Social**: Weekly creation snippet (short video/audio clip)
2. **Communities**: Join 3 creator communities
   - Post works for critique
   - Engage in other creators' feedback
   - Collaborate on 2-3 projects

3. **Collections/Blog**: Monthly "behind the scenes" post
   - Process notes
   - Learnings from feedback
   - Next direction

#### Advanced Feedback Loop
1. **Automation**: Monthly analysis
   - Which works got best feedback?
   - Which feedback types do you act on most?
   - Predict success of new work based on feedback patterns
   
2. **Create**: Version all works
   - v1 (raw/draft)
   - v2 (feedback incorporated)
   - v3 (polished)
   - Track iteration count

#### Collaboration
1. **Communities**: Join collab requests
   - "Looking for vocalist for track"
   - "Need design review"
   - **Automation**: Suggests collabs matching your skill level

2. **Teams**: If collaborating with 1-2 people:
   - Create shared workspace
   - Communities channel for discussion
   - Shared Productivity tasks
   - Real-time feedback in Create

### Year 2 Output
- 156 more creative sessions
- 38 finished works (improvement from Y1's 12)
- 80+ social posts (growing audience)
- 5-10 published collaborations
- Communities reputation (respected critiques)

### Power User Techniques

**1. AI-Assisted Feedback Aggregation**
```
Automation weekly:
├─ Collect all community feedback comments
├─ Tag feedback by type: technical, conceptual, emotional, structural
├─ Creates task with template: "Consider feedback on [work_name]"
│  - Technical notes (linked and bolded)
│  - Conceptual notes
│  - etc.
└─ You review aggregated feedback (5 min vs 30 min scanning threads)
```

**2. Mood → Creativity Correlation**
- Health logs mood + creativity level
- Automation detects pattern: "You're most creative when..."
  - Well-rested?
  - Social the day before?
  - After exercise?
  - Certain time of day?
- Automations optimize schedule based on patterns

**3. Iterative Version Tracking**
```
Create workspace for single work:
├─ v1_draft (initial idea)
├─ v1_feedback_notes (comments + suggestions)
├─ v2_incorporated (feedback applied)
├─ v2_feedback_round2 (new round)
├─ v3_polished (final)
└─ v3_published (released)

Automation compares versions; shows what changed from feedback
```

**4. Output Metrics**
```
Productivity tracks:
├─ Hours logged per week (creeping toward 10-15 hrs/week)
├─ Works completed per month
├─ Feedback incorporation rate (% of feedback applied)
└─ Community engagement (reactions, collabs initiated)

Health tracks:
├─ Flow state frequency (hours lost in work)
├─ Creative energy (confidence + excitement)
└─ Burnout metrics (fatigue, frustration)

Balance workflow: if burnout rising, reduce tasks next week
```

#### Result
- 2 years = 312 creative hours
- 50 finished works (average 6.2 per work)
- Publishable portfolio
- Engaged community
- Clear trajectory (Y2 quality >> Y1 quality)
- Documented creative process (timeline visible in Create)

## Scenario 5: Life-Tracking Dashboard (Full Data Autonomy)

**Goal**: Create a personal data infrastructure where all your life data flows into one coherent dashboard, accessible anywhere, queryable, and under your complete control.

### Architecture Setup

#### Local Storage Foundation
1. **All universes** store everything locally (encrypted)
2. **Sync strategy**: You control what syncs to cloud
3. **Access**: Web, mobile, desktop (same data everywhere)

#### Unified Query System
1. **Universal search** works across all universes:
   - "Show me all creative work from Q3"
   - "What was my mood during the mountain trip?"
   - "How much did I spend on coffee this year?"
   - "List all skills I learned in 2025"

2. **Advanced queries** (power users):
   - Correlations: "Mood vs. hours of creating?"
   - Trends: "Sleep quality trajectory over 6 months?"
   - Predictions: "If I sleep 8 hrs tonight + exercise tomorrow AM, will I be in top 25% creative mood?"

#### Dashboard Creation
1. **Create**: Build personal dashboard (shareable or private)
   - Widgets showing:
     - Year-to-date productivity (tasks completed)
     - Health trends (sleep, mood, exercise)
     - Financial snapshot (spending by category)
     - Creative output (works created, finished)
     - Learning progress (courses, hours, certifications)
     - Travel distance (miles, countries, cities)
     - Social impact (posts, helps given, friends made)

2. **Enable automations**:
   - Dashboard auto-updates weekly
   - Monthly deep-dive report auto-generated (PDF)
   - Year-end review compiled automatically

### Power Techniques

**1. Privacy Vault Pattern**
```
You decide per-universe: Who can see what?

PUBLIC (no login):
- Social: Public posts visible
- Communities: Public threads visible

FRIENDS ONLY:
- Social: Friend-only posts
- Travel: Friends see trip photos
- Create: Collaborators see works

PRIVATE (encryption):
- Health: Only you (end-to-end encrypted server, or local-only)
- Finance: Only you (encrypted even if synced)
- Learning: Only you (progress private)

Each universe has independent privacy; you control each.
```

**2. Data Portability**
- Export all personal data (what you own):
  - Finance: CSV of all transactions
  - Health: Full dataset (JSON or CSV)
  - Travel: All journeys with photos
  - Creative: All works (files + metadata)
  - Learning: Transcript + course data
- One-click "Export My Life" downloads year's data

**3. Temporal Analysis**
```
Automation runs monthly:
├─ Compare this month vs. last month
│  ├─ Productivity: Tasks done? (↑ or ↓)
│  ├─ Health: Sleep quality? (trend)
│  ├─ Finance: Spending? (budget adherence)
│  ├─ Learning: Hours? (consistency)
│  └─ Social: Engagement? (quality of interactions)
│
└─ Quarterly comparison
   └─ Yearly comparison
   └─ Trends emerge
```

**4. Predictive Wellness**
- Health + Productivity + Sleep data feeds AI
- "Based on your patterns, you'll have a good productivity week if you:"
  - Get > 7 hrs sleep tonight
  - Exercise tomorrow AM
  - Have a social interaction (friend visit/call)
- You try the suggestions; log if they worked
- AI learns your personal optimizers

#### Result
- Complete life dashboard (unified)
- All your data, under your control
- Portable (can export anytime)
- Queryable (ask questions, get answers)
- Predictive (anticipate good/bad weeks)
- Privacy-first (you decide visibility of each piece)
- No proprietary lock-in (your data stays yours)

## Advanced Power User Tips

### 1. Keyboard Mastery
- Learn all universe shortcuts cold
- Practice context switching (jump between universes in 2 seconds)
- Use Companion Panel keyboard nav (arrow keys, Enter, Esc)
- Result: 2x faster workflow

### 2. Automation Composition
- Start with simple workflows (1-2 steps)
- Chain them together (workflow output = next workflow input)
- Share templates with team
- Result: Complex automation built incrementally

### 3. Data-Driven Decisions
- Before major decision, ask for data from relevant universe
  - "Should I take this travel gig?" → Check Finance, Health, Productivity, Social
  - "Am I ready to launch this project?" → Check Create version history (maturity), Communities feedback, Finance (budget)
- Dashboard shows cross-universe impact

### 4. Community Leverage
- Join communities related to your work
- Contribute (answer questions, give feedback)
- Ask for help (specific, not vague)
- Result: Network grows, expertise recognized

### 5. Continuous Learning Loop
- Learning universe picks up subskills as you encounter gaps
- "You need X skill; here's a course" (automation suggests)
- Apply immediately in Create
- Communities validates (positive feedback accelerates confidence)
