# Start Here: Welcome to Aurora

Aurora is a calm, beautiful, emotionally intelligent digital civilization. It unifies 15 universes into one coherent experience and adapts to your context with a companion system.

## The Aurora Promise
- Calm, not chaotic
- Beautiful, not cluttered
- Intelligent, not intrusive
- Human-first, not metric-first

## What Aurora Is
A single platform for creativity, learning, productivity, health, finance, social connection, commerce, and more. Each universe has its own tools and tone, but all universes share the same design language and ethics.

## What Aurora Is Not
- Not an algorithmic feed machine
- Not an ad network or data broker
- Not a tool that forces notifications
- Not a one-size-fits-all interface

## Core Principles You Will Feel Immediately
- Local-first by default
- Clear controls for memory and suggestions
- Predictable navigation across universes
- Accessibility baked into every surface

## The 15 Universes (Quick Summary)
- Home: Your daily dashboard and orientation
- Learning: Courses, progress, and skill growth
- Create: Creative studio for writing, design, and building
- Productivity: Tasks, goals, and focus
- Health and Wellness: Habits, health logs, and wellbeing
- Finance: Budgeting, spending, and goals
- Social: Feeds, messages, and communities
- Entertainment: Media discovery and relaxation
- Travel: Trip planning and journals
- Home Control: Smart home management
- Automation: Workflows and rules
- Commerce: Shopping and purchasing
- Brand: Identity and campaigns
- Communities: Groups, roles, and events
- Gaming: Library, highlights, and tournaments

## The Companion System (In One Minute)
Aurora adapts its tone based on where you are and what you are doing. The companion uses:
- Tone: Mentor, Strategist, Explorer, Analyst, Guardian
- Scope: Minimal, Light, Standard, Deep, Intensive
- Signals: Focus, Exploration, Learning, Creation, Relaxation, Crisis, Collaboration, Reflection

For full details, see [docs/aurora-book/end-user/companion-system.md](docs/aurora-book/end-user/companion-system.md).

## Where To Find Key Controls
- Theme and mode: top navigation theme selector
- Companion Panel: CP button in top navigation
- Search: global search in top navigation
- Docs: help button in top navigation
- Accessibility: settings panel and system preferences

## Quick Start
1. Open Home and review your dashboard.
2. Choose one universe to explore.
3. Open the Companion Panel (CP) to see tone and scope.
4. Set your theme (light, dark, illuminated, or system).
5. Enable memory if you want Aurora to remember context.

## Recommended First Universes
- Home: orientation and daily summary
- Learning: clear progress and structure
- Create: quick creative wins
- Productivity: goals and focus
