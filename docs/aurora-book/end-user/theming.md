# Theming and Personalization

Aurora adapts to your aesthetic preferences with modes, families, and accessibility adjustments.

## Theme Modes
- Light: High contrast for daytime
- Dark: Reduced eye strain
- Illuminated: Aurora signature glow and color
- System: Follows your OS preference

## Theme Families
- Home, Office, Outdoor, Lifestyle, Creative

## Customization
- Font size
- Spacing density
- Motion (reduce animations)
- Contrast (high contrast mode)
- Accent color

Settings persist across sessions and can sync if you opt in.

## Where To Change Themes
- Theme selector in top navigation
- Accessibility settings in the Settings universe

## Recommended Combinations
- Focus work: Dark + Office
- Creative sessions: Illuminated + Creative
- Outdoor planning: Light + Outdoor

## Motion and Comfort
- If you prefer minimal motion, enable reduced motion at the OS level
- Aurora respects system settings and reduces animated effects

## Troubleshooting
- Theme not saving: confirm memory or preferences are enabled
- Colors look washed out: disable high contrast if it is on
