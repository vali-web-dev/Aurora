# Home Universe

## Purpose
Your digital hearth. Home is the orientation and summary center for your day.

## What It Is
- Central dashboard and welcome center
- Recent activity and quick stats
- Personalized suggestions from other universes
- Calm starting point for each session

## Who Uses It
- Anyone starting their day
- Users who want a summary view
- People taking a break or resetting focus

## Companion Tone
Mentor (warm, welcoming, oriented to you)

## Key Features
- Welcome message that adapts to time of day
- Quick links to favorite universes
- Unread counts and cross-universe updates
- Calm, breathing interface with minimal noise

## Core Surfaces
- Home dashboard (summary cards and recent activity)
- Quick stats and cross-universe signals
- Suggested next actions
- Theme and companion entry points

## Key Actions
- Review your day and recent activity
- Jump into a universe with one click
- Open the Companion Panel (CP) for guidance
- Adjust theme and accessibility settings

## Signals and Personalization
- Focus signal reduces surface noise
- Relaxation signal softens visuals and suggestions
- Exploration signal surfaces more universe suggestions

## Notifications
- Summaries only; no urgent alerts by default
- Notification counts are local and respect privacy settings

## Step-by-step Guides

### Review Your Day
1. Open the Home dashboard.
2. Scan summary cards for updates.
3. Open CP to see tone and scope.

### Jump to a Universe
1. Use a quick link, or open global search.
2. Select a universe and continue.

### Reset Context
1. Open CP.
2. Clear recent memory items.

### Access Quick Settings
1. Open Home dashboard.
2. Click Settings icon (bottom right).
3. Adjust quick toggles (theme, do not disturb, notifications).
4. Changes apply immediately.

### Customize Dashboard Layout
1. Click Edit Layout (or drag mode).
2. Reorder summary cards.
3. Add or remove quick links.
4. Arrange in sections that match your workflow.
5. Save when done.

### Check System Status
1. Open System Status widget.
2. View connected device status.
3. Check notification queue.
4. See quick health indicators (battery, storage, etc.).

## Visual Guide

**Dashboard Summary View**
> Shows your Home universe dashboard with summary cards arranged by area: quick links, recent universes, CP suggestions, and today's quick wins. Key elements: Card grid layout, color-coded status indicators, quick action buttons on each card.

**Quick Links Panel**
> Sidebar or collapsible panel showing personalized quick links to other universes. Shows 6-8 most frequent destinations with icons and labels. Key elements: Universe icons, optional badges showing unread count or urgent items.

**Companion Panel Integration**
> Companion Panel docked on Home showing context from your recent actions. Displays tone selector, current scope breadcrumb, and active signals. Key elements: Tone button, scope hierarchy, signal badges, status indicator (listening/ready).

## How To
- Review your day: open Home and scan the summary cards.
- Jump to a universe: use quick links or global search.
- Reset your context: open CP and clear the recent memory list.

## Common Workflows
- Morning review then jump to Productivity
- Quick check-in and then Social
- Evening recap and theme adjustment

## Privacy Notes
Home shows summaries based on local memory unless you disable memory.

## Accessibility Notes
- Full keyboard navigation and visible focus
- Screen reader friendly headings and summaries
- Reduced motion support for calm mode
