## Step-by-step Guides

### Discover and Start Watching
1. Open Entertainment and browse by genre or trending.
2. Click on a title to see details.
3. Click Watch or Read.
4. Content starts where you left off.

### Rate and Share a Review
1. After watching, click Rate.
2. Select your rating (stars or thumbs).
3. Click Write Review (optional).
4. Share to Social if desired.

### Host a Watch Party
1. Click on a title and select Watch Party.
2. Invite friends by link or contacts.
3. They join an in-sync viewing session.
4. Chat in real-time alongside playback.

### Create a Watchlist Collection
1. Click Watchlist (or Library).
2. Click New Collection.
3. Name it (e.g., "Must Watch", "Rainy Days").
4. Add items from discovery.
5. Organize and reorder.

### Continue from Where You Left Off
1. Open Entertainment or Library.
2. View In Progress or Recently Watched.
3. Click to resume at exact timestamp.
4. Continue watching or reading.

### Get Personalized Recommendations
1. Ensure recommendations are enabled.
2. Rate items you watch.
3. View Recommended For You section.
4. Recommendations improve over time.
5. Adjust recommendation settings anytime.

## Visual Guide

**Entertainment Homepage**
> Browse view with genre categories, recommended titles, and watchlist. Shows content in rows by genre or curated lists (Trending, New, Favorites). Key elements: Content cards with cover images and title overlays, genre tabs, search bar, watchlist quick-access.

**Title Details & Playback**
> Full details for a single title including synopsis, cast, rating, and playbback player. Shows media player with timeline scrubber, subtitles/audio options. Key elements: Media player with playback controls, title/description, rating, next episode button (if series), download option.

**Watch Party with Chat**
> Shared viewing experience showing content player and side-by-side chat. Participants see same media with synchronized playback. Key elements: Media player taking 70% width, chat column on right showing messages from party members with timestamps, live participant count.
