# Aurora Quick Reference

*A 5-minute orientation to Aurora's core concepts.*

---

## What Is Aurora?

A 15-universe digital world where each space has its own personality. One tool for creativity, learning, productivity, health, finance, and more. Everything local, private, and under your control.

## The 5 Tones

Aurora adapts its personality based on where you are:

| Tone | Feels Like | Found In | Greeting |
|------|-----------|----------|----------|
| 🧑‍🏫 **Mentor** | Warm, encouraging | Learning, Home | "What are you working on today?" |
| 🎯 **Strategist** | Direct, goal-focused | Productivity, Commerce | "Let's organize your goals." |
| 🔍 **Explorer** | Spirited, curious | Create, Social, Travel | "What sparks your curiosity?" |
| 📊 **Analyst** | Neutral, data-driven | Finance, Developer, AI | "Here's what's happening." |
| 🛡️ **Guardian** | Protective, safe | Health, Security | "Your safety comes first." |

## Companion Panel (CP)

Click **CP** in the top navigation to see:
- **Current Tone:** How Aurora is thinking right now
- **Current Scope:** What tools/memory are available
- **Theme:** What Aurora is focusing on
- **Active Signals:** Your current mood/mode
- **Memory Controls:** Toggle on/off, clear all
- **Quick Notes:** Capture thoughts (saved locally)

## Signals: Set Your Mood

Click signals to tell Aurora how you're feeling:

- 🎓 **Learning** → Mentor tone, examples everywhere
- 🎯 **Focus** → Strategist tone, quiet, minimal distractions
- 🔍 **Exploration** → Explorer tone, more options visible
- 🎨 **Creation** → Remove friction, lean into flow
- 😌 **Relaxation** → Gentle, calm, no pressure
- 📢 **Collaboration** → Team mode, shared context
- 🤝 **Reflection** → Show me insights and patterns
- 🚨 **Crisis** → Guardian escalates, full support

## Themes (🎨 button)

**Modes:** Light, Dark, Illuminated, System  
**Families:** Home (warm blue), Office (professional), Outdoor (green), Lifestyle (pink), Creative (purple)

Switch anytime. Syncs across your devices.

## Memory Works Local-Only

- Aurora remembers where you've been (if you enable it)
- Everything stored in your browser (not on a server)
- You can turn memory off or clear it anytime
- Optional sync to your account for multi-device backup

**Data Aurora Never Touches:**
- Passwords (except in encrypted vault)
- Private messages
- Health data (unless you export it)
- Anything you don't want shared

## Accessibility Built-In

Aurora works for everyone:
- ♿ Keyboard navigation (Tab, Enter, Esc)
- 👁️ Screen readers fully supported
- 🎨 High contrast + color blindness modes
- 📝 Large text option
- 🎬 Reduced motion (respects your OS preference)

## Privacy in 60 Seconds

- **Default:** Memory OFF, suggestions OFF
- **You control:** Everything (toggle memory, clear data, opt-in to features)
- **We promise:** No selling data, no ads, no tracking outside your browser
- **Portability:** Export everything anytime, leave anytime

## Getting Started

1. **Pick a universe** you're curious about
2. **Click CP** to meet your companion
3. **Click 🎨** to adjust your theme
4. **Turn on memory** if you want Aurora to remember you
5. **Explore** at your own pace

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Tab` | Navigate between elements |
| `Enter` | Activate/select |
| `Esc` | Close modals, exit forms |
| `↑ ↓` | Navigate lists |
| `Ctrl+K` or `S` | Search (mobile) |
| `Ctrl+/` | Open documentation portal |
| `Ctrl+B` | Toggle Companion Panel |
| `Ctrl+H` | Go to Home |
| `Ctrl+Shift+L` | Go to Learning |
| `Ctrl+Shift+F` | Go to Create |
| `Ctrl+Shift+P` | Go to Productivity |
| `?` | Keyboard shortcuts help |
| `🎨` | Theme selector (top nav) |
| `CP` | Companion panel |
| `🔔` | Notifications |
